## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an enchanting, delicate, and deeply romantic floral boutique aesthetic. Built around the tactile warmth of fresh blossoms and celebratory moments, it merges organic femininity with modern, highly structured e-commerce clarity. 

The experience targets shoppers seeking curated floral arrangements, bouquets for milestone occasions, and thoughtful gifts. The visual language conveys emotional tenderness, refinement, and immediate ease:
- **Style Direction:** Soft Romantic Modernism. Generous pastel backdrops, pure white floating containers, delicate hairline botanical/butterfly illustrations, and softly rounded silhouettes.
- **Atmospheric Tone:** Sweet yet sophisticated, avoiding overly vintage clutter in favor of clean layouts, airy negative space, and gentle transitions.
- **Conversion Intent:** Warm, highly reliable call-to-actions, with specialized emphasis on direct ordering, personalized card messaging, and conversational checkout via WhatsApp.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from petal nuances, berry tones, and botanical freshness, grounded by a rich, warm charcoal-plum tone for high-contrast legibility.

- **Canvas & Background:** The overarching page background uses soft blush pink (`#FDE4E6`), instilling warmth across every view.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#C9567A`):** Raspberry pink represents brand recognition, primary buttons, price tags, and interactive links.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8B1E4A`):** Deep burgundy adds depth, editorial sophistication, active states, and refined section headers.
- **Conversion Accent (`#25D366`):** Vibrant WhatsApp green, reserved strictly for instant checkout, catalog inquiries, and direct messaging actions to maximize contrast and user confidence.
- **Surface & Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white cards float effortlessly above the blush canvas, maintaining product photography fidelity and pristine readability.
- **Neutral / Text (`#3A2A30`):** Warm espresso plum replaces harsh black, providing comfortable contrast that harmonizes naturally with the pink tonal spectrum.

## Typography

Quicksand serves as the cornerstone typography across headlines, UI controls, and body text. Its rounded geometry reinforces the soft, friendly, and welcoming temperament of the boutique, while retaining crisp legibility across dense product grids and mobile screen sizes.

- **Editorial Accents & Callouts:** Delicate script accents (such as hero eyebrow tags, custom greeting card previews, or floral collection sub-notes) are applied sparingly using handwritten fluid glyphs to mirror the brand's signature mark.
- **Hierarchy & Tracking:** Titles use bold or semi-bold weights with gentle negative tracking (`-0.01em`) to create a cohesive editorial finish, while labels and small captions utilize subtle open tracking (`+0.02em`) for effortless scannability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a breathable fluid grid that gives flower arrangements, bouquets, and occasion-based gifts room to shine.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Mobile: 4 columns with 1.25rem gutters and 1.5rem exterior margins.
  - Tablet: 8 columns with 1.5rem gutters and 2rem exterior margins.
  - Desktop: 12 columns with a max content container of 1240px, 2rem gutters, and 3rem exterior padding.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm between sections (`space-xl` to `4rem`) prevents visual clutter and allows delicate fine-line butterfly motifs to float freely between cards and headers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, tinted ambient drop shadows that simulate natural daylight gently diffusing over soft petal textures. Surfaces never use clinical gray shadows.

- **Canvas Tier:** The base layer sits at `#FDE4E6`.
- **Card Surfaces:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` panels elevate subtly using a warm plum-tinted drop shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(139, 30, 74, 0.08)`.
- **Interactive Hover & Modals:** Elevated elements gently lift with a double-layered shadow: `0px 14px 32px -6px rgba(139, 30, 74, 0.12), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(139, 30, 74, 0.06)`.
- **Delicate Outlines:** Structural borders on inactive fields or subtle dividers use a soft 1px wash of `#C9567A` at 15% opacity (`rgba(201, 86, 122, 0.15)`), keeping outlines soft and organic.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a 20px (`1.25rem`) standard radius for cards, primary interactive elements, and image containers, matching the soft, curved nature of floral petals.

- **Cards & Modals:** Uniform 20px border radius with smoothly clipped image banners.
- **Buttons & Tags:** Fully pill-shaped or soft 20px rounded boundaries to provide a friendly, inviting touch target.
- **Motifs:** Fine-line contour illustrations of butterflies and botanicals (0.75px to 1px stroke weight) appear as decorative background flourishes or subtle stamps over cards.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary (Brand):* Solid `#C9567A` background, pure white text, 20px rounded radius, soft raspberry focus ring. On hover, smooth transition to deep burgundy `#8B1E4A`.
  - *Conversion CTA (WhatsApp):* Solid `#25D366` background, `#FFFFFF` bold text with a 20px or pill border, accompanied by the WhatsApp icon for seamless direct purchase flow.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#C9567A`, text in `#8B1E4A`.
- **Product Cards:**
  - Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with 20px rounded corners, subtle warm plum shadow, and high-aspect ratio product imagery with gentle zoom on hover.
  - Price tags rendered in bold `#C9567A`, bouquet tags (e.g., "Rosas", "Aniversario") displayed as soft blush pills.
- **Form Inputs & Dedication Fields:**
  - White background, 20px border-radius, soft pink border (`rgba(201, 86, 122, 0.2)`). Focus state illuminates with a crisp `#C9567A` outline and faint halo.
  - Dedication card text areas include delicate butterfly watermark details in the lower-right corner.
- **Badges & Filter Chips:**
  - Inactive: `#FFFFFF` background with plum-tinted borders and `#3A2A30` text.
  - Active: `#C9567A` background with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography.
- **Interactive Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Smooth rounded checkboxes and circular radios filled with `#C9567A` when selected, finished with smooth micro-animations.
